Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about building your dream home? We've answered the most common ones below.

How long does it take to build a 3BHK villa?
On average, a premium 3BHK villa takes between 10 to 14 months depending on the design complexity, weather conditions, and site accessibility. We provide a detailed timeline during the planning phase and send weekly progress updates.
Do you assist with government approvals and permits?
Yes, we provide end-to-end assistance for HMDA/GHMC approvals, building permits, and all necessary documentation. Our legal team handles the paperwork so you can focus on your vision.
What kind of warranty do you provide?
We offer a comprehensive 10-year structural warranty and a 1-year maintenance warranty for all our residential projects. This covers foundation, walls, roofing, plumbing, and electrical systems.
What is the cost per square foot for construction?
Our construction packages start from ₹1,800 per sq.ft for standard builds and go up to ₹3,500 per sq.ft for premium luxury finishes. The exact cost depends on the design, materials chosen, and site conditions. We provide a free, no-obligation estimate after the initial site visit.
Can I make changes during construction?
Yes, minor design modifications can be accommodated during the early stages of construction. Major structural changes may involve additional costs and timeline adjustments. We always keep you informed before any changes are made.
Do you build on my own land or provide plots?
We specialize in "Build on Your Land" services. If you already own a plot in Hyderabad or surrounding areas, we handle everything from design to handover. We can also assist in finding suitable plots through our partner network.
